YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy & Digital Safety

Digital safety is an important part of personal safety.

Privacy Guidelines

🔒 Avoid saving sensitive information on shared devices.
📱 Be aware that device monitoring software may expose your activities.
🌐 Browser history and network activity may remain visible.
🔑 Use trusted devices whenever possible.
🛡️ Avoid sharing personal information with unknown websites.
Quick Exit only navigates away from this website. It does not erase browser history, network records, screenshots, downloads or monitoring traces. Use a safer device if you believe yours is being monitored.
